# Hi, I'm Aldo Carletta 👋

**Hydraulic engineer** and **PhD candidate at the University of Catania**, working on digital water networks: GIS, SCADA telecontrol, smart metering and hydraulic modelling. My research focuses on the assessment and mitigation of urban and territorial risks.

🗺️ **Interactive geospatial CV:** [ing-aldo-c.github.io](https://ing-aldo-c.github.io/) — my career on a map, in Italian and English.

## What I work on

- **GIS & WebGIS** — georeferenced asset inventories, field mapping, web maps
- **SCADA & telecontrol** — architecture and monitoring of water networks and plants
- **Smart metering** — LoRaWAN networks and remote meter reading
- **Water loss reduction** — district metering, pressure management, water balances
- **Hydraulic modelling** — water distribution networks and river hydraulics
- **OT cybersecurity** — NIS2 compliance for operational technology
